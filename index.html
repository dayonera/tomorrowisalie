Inside the book

Five rules that get you off the couch

RULE 01

Make it stupidly small

Big goals scare your brain. Tiny actions sneak past it. “Run 5 km” becomes “put on your shoes.”

RULE 02

Move first

Motivation comes after, not before. Feelings are weather. Actions are direction.

RULE 03

Kill the friction

Make the good stuff easy and the bad stuff annoying. You need fewer obstacles, not more discipline.

RULE 04

Become the person first

Every time you follow through, you cast a vote for who you're becoming.

RULE 05

Stack it

Attach the new action to something you already do. “After I brush my teeth, I do 10 push-ups.”
